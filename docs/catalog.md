# Catalog Snapshot

- Categories: Beverages, Condiments, Confections, Dairy Products, Grains/Cereals, Meat/Poultry, Produce, Seafood.
- Products map to categories as in the Northwind DB.

## Reporting groups
- For management reporting, Grains/Cereals and Produce are combined into a single reporting group named "Pantry".
- Every other reporting group is identical to its category.
